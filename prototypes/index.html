Social Jackets

A community page with public member profiles, a newest-first persistent feed, a text composer, and sourced campus events. Authors can edit or delete their posts and link existing resources or events. Geographic maps remain pending.

Open Social Jackets

Social Jackets and Thermal share the local FastAPI/PostgreSQL backend. Public posting requires an account and a publicly shared profile; private profile fields and uploads stay private. Both interfaces load CDN libraries and need internet access.

Open prototypes/README.md for run instructions and prototype limitations.
